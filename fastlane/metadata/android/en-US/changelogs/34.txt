Wisp 2.8.3 — fewer permissions.

• RECORD_AUDIO, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE are no longer declared. They came from the camera plugin behind the QR scanner, which supports video capture; Wisp only reads frames to decode a code and never requested them at runtime.

This restores the permission set the app had before the scanner changed in 2.8.1.
